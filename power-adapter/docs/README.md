# Power adapter / Adaptador de alimentación

Published adapter envelopes, with explicitly generic interface and presentation
features. The body is a rounded fit surface; it is not an electronics replica.

## Frame and modes

| Catalog axis | Model |
|---|---|
| body width | X |
| body depth | Y |
| body height | Z, floor at zero |

`device` displays the body and output cord as distinct parts. `body` includes
archetype label/indicator recesses, optional inlet and wall-adapter prongs.
`cord` is a generic straight route, tapered boot and output connector envelope.
`envelope` is the plain body only, with exactly the published body bounds; it
excludes prongs and cord. This is the surface a case or fixture consumes.

Components `adapter_body`, `adapter_prongs`, `adapter_inlet` live in the new
`power_cases` module. `connector_plug` and `strain_relief` reuse `cables`.
`family.py` is authored; `main.py` is generated by `tools/inline_parts.py`.

## Facts and fallback rules

The generator reads only sourced `dimensions_mm.states.body`, cord length and
output identity from the keystone. The Switch, Switch 2 and Wii adapters have
body presets; GameCube and Famicom adapters await sourced body dimensions.
Published axes are preserved, including any unresolved projecting-parts caveat.

The corpus does not source prong geometry or adapter form. Those remain generic
and are listed as fallbacks. The default is an inline body; four archetype
presets demonstrate inline, flat-prong, round-prong and larger-brick forms.
These pattern names do not assert a national mains standard. The animation
changes the length of generic prongs, not a sourced folding mechanism.

IEC C8/C14 recess width/height and USB-C/DC mating dimensions come from the
standard-parts catalog at SPEC_PIN. Unpublished housing/depth values remain
archetypes. The label is only a shallow pocket, with no artwork. Electrical
ratings stay in the reference record and do not become geometry controls.

A supplied cable may be detachable; the preview stages it as a generic attached
route, never a claim about the manufacturer's construction or tip-to-tip length.
A missing output identity becomes a generic unlabelled endpoint. The body-only
fit surface remains useful independently of those unresolved features.
