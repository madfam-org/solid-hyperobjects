# Console Dock

An original docking/charging family. Named presets carry **sourced outer body
envelopes and covered port/capacity counts**; the profile, device seat, positions,
openings and service cover remain explicit archetype details. A named preset is
not a manufacturer's shell replica. See [NOTICE](../NOTICE).

## Frame and source mapping

X is body width, Y depth (front negative), Z height above Z=0. Published
`dimensions_mm.states.body` width/depth/height maps directly to X/Y/Z. The
`envelope` is a closed body hull independent of moving cover poses. It describes
body dimensions, not proof that a physical device fits the archetype seat.

| Named preset | Sourced W × D × H (mm) |
|---|---|
| Nintendo Switch dock | 173 × 54 × 104 |
| Nintendo Switch dock with LAN port | 175 × 50.7 × 104 |
| Nintendo Switch 2 dock | 201 × 51.2 × 115 |
| DualSense charging station CFI-ZDS1 | 52 × 159 × 62 |

The complete source records, dates, quotes and conflicting evidence live in the
keystone catalog at `SPEC_PIN`. The generator includes their family digest and
fallback groups in each preset. USB kinds remain generic when the source does
not distinguish the connector type; source side/rear positions are preserved in
the catalog, while this model's grouped rear opening layout is archetypal.

## Modes and components

| Mode | Part | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| dock | dock | chassis, optional boss and optional separate cover |
| envelope | envelope | one body hull at published W×D×H |
| rear_door | rear_door | independent printable cover variant, even if not selected in assembly |

The assembled dock is one solid when no cover is selected, two with a cover.
The cover can rotate 0–90°. Its existence and construction are not sourced facts
about a named product. Charging-station presets omit the boss and cover rather
than imply a USB-C contact layout. The independent cover mode is a design option.

`family.py` is authored; `main.py` is generated by
`python3 tools/inline_parts.py --write console-dock`. Shared `console_supports`
components supply chassis, envelope cuts, bosses, generic port cuts and cover.
Interfaces describe device envelope, cradle seat, connector boss and cover axis.
They record design intent, not mating or collision proof.

## Archetype rules

A body narrower than its depth selects the charging-station construction; other
bodies select a tablet cradle. That selection is our rule from envelope ratios,
not a manufacturer classification. Unknown capacity falls back explicitly.
The default is 180×80×110 mm, and a second original two-seat charger is
65×170×60 mm; neither asserts a product.

Wall 2 mm, base min(12 mm,20% height), corner radius 4% of the smaller footprint
axis, and clearance 0.4 mm are our rules. Seats take 86% of tablet-cradle width
or 72% of charging-station width, 24% of body depth and 90% of height. Cradle
angle is 10°, charging-station angle 0°. These sizes are not inferred device
measurements. USB-C boss shell dimensions come from the keystone standard part;
its connector role/position remains an explicitly named fallback. Generic USB,
HDMI, Ethernet and power openings are original keep-outs, not connector specs.

## Awaiting a source

Actual host-only envelopes, cradle fit, precise connector role/position,
clearance, shell profiles, port openings, cover and hinge details remain
unverified. No source about an overall dock size proves these details. Physical
retention, docking contacts, electrical operation, collision-free motion and
manufacturing feasibility are not certified by this geometry.
