# Disc Media

A game disc as a parametric **archetype**: one revolved profile (diameter, centre
hole, thickness, clamp zone, stacking ring), optionally carried in a shell with a
window, a read slot and a sliding shutter: the disc-in-shell formats. CadQuery
(B-Rep) reference. Read [`../NOTICE`](../NOTICE): a preset is a fit envelope, not
a likeness.

## Frame — the keystone standard parts' own

| Model | Source | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `disc_diameter`, `disc_hole`, `disc_thickness` | standard part `diameter`, `centre_hole_diameter`, `substrate_thickness` / `thickness`; or reference `features.diameter_mm`, `hole_mm`, `thickness_mm` | the disc lies in XY, its hole at the origin; z = 0 is the read-out side, +Z runs to the label side |
| `clamp_inner_diameter`, `clamp_outer_diameter` | the standard's clamping-zone inner maximum / outer minimum | the clamp zone |
| `ring_height` | standard total height max − thickness (**derived**) | the stacking ring, so the disc fills the standard's own envelope solid |
| `shell_width`, `shell_length`, `shell_thickness` | `umd-cartridge` `case_width`, `case_length`, `case_thickness`; or reference `states.shell.width`, `.height`, `.depth` | X, Y, Z of the shell, centred on its bounding rectangle, standing on z = 0 |
| `shell_boss_*` | ECMA-365's 4.35 mm maximum inside the D1 = 50 mm circle | the thicker zone about the disc centre |
| `disc_center_y` | `case_length / 2 − top_arc_radius` (**derived**: the top arc is "about the disc") | the disc's place in the shell |

The disc is **one revolved profile** on `Workplane("XZ")` revolved about local
(0, 1, 0), the global Z axis (the revolve-axis rule). The arched outline is one
2-D wire of lines and three-point arcs, so no tangent solids are booleaned.

## Modes and parts

| Mode | Parts | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `disc` | disc | the revolved disc at the origin |
| `shell` | shell, shutter, loaded_disc | the shell (pocket, label window, spindle hole, radial read slot, shutter track), the shutter plate and the disc in its pocket. Always built from the shell parameters. |
| `envelope` | envelope | **one body**: the shell's hull with its boss when `shell_present`, otherwise the disc's cylinder at its diameter and maximum height (the standard parts' envelope solid; the hole is not cut) |

Every (mode, part) is one body. Animation: `shutter-open` (`shutter_open` 0 → 1,
mode `shell`): the plate slides a full plate width in +X along its track.

## Composition

Components are canonical text in
[`commons-lib/hardware/disc_media.py`](../../commons-lib/hardware/disc_media.py),
**inlined at build** (ruling R2). `family.py` is authored; `main.py` is generated by
`python3 tools/inline_parts.py --write disc-media`.

| Component | CDG exposed |
|---|---|
| `revolved_disc` | `centre_hole`, `outline` (the standard parts' interface ids) |
| `disc_shell_outline`, `disc_shell_boss` | `media_envelope` |
| `disc_pocket`, `disc_shutter_track` | — (voids) |
| `disc_window` | `disc_window` |
| `disc_read_slot` | `read_window` |
| `disc_shutter_plate` | `media_shutter` |

`centre_hole` and `outline` are `compatible_with` jewel-case, keep-case,
media-caddy and home-console; `media_envelope` also with slab-handheld.

## Presets

Generated by `tools/presets/presets.py` (module `tools/presets/disc_media.py`);
CI runs `--check-all`, and a standard part whose facts move changes its preset, so
the check fails until the presets are regenerated.

| Preset | Kind | Mode | Envelope X × Y × Z (mm) | Source | Falls back to the archetype |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `standard-optical-disc-cd-120mm` | standard_part | disc | 120 × 120 × 1.5 | ECMA-130 via `optical-disc-cd-120mm` | — (ring width, place: ours) |
| `standard-optical-disc-dvd-120mm` | standard_part | disc | 120 × 120 × 1.5 | ECMA-267 via `optical-disc-dvd-120mm` | — (ring width, place: ours) |
| `standard-umd-cartridge` | standard_part | shell | 64 × 65 × 4.35 | ECMA-365 via `umd-cartridge` | centre hole, clamp zone, window, read slot, shutter |
| `archetype-square-shell-disc` (= the default) | archetype | shell | 80 × 84 × 5 | ours | everything |

The reference catalog files no `disc-media` object yet. A catalog object becomes
a preset with its diameter, hole and thickness, and for a shelled format its
`states.shell` as a rounded rectangle with the disc centred.

## Archetype rules (ours)

- Stacking ring 1 mm wide, on the label side, starting at the clamp zone's outer
  diameter; a catalog disc's ring is 0 (its published thickness is the maximum).
- Unsourced centre hole and clamp zone scale with the diameter from the 120 mm
  standards (15/120, 26/120, 33/120).
- Pocket clearance 0.3 mm; the disc is centred in the shell's thickness.
- Window 50 % × 25 % of the diameter, 20 % of the diameter toward the top;
  read slot 17 % × 30 %, starting 1 mm outside a 17 % spindle hole; shutter
  overlap 2 mm, plate 0.5 mm in a 0.55 mm track that runs one plate width in +X.
- Rounded-rectangle shell corners 3 mm when unsourced.
