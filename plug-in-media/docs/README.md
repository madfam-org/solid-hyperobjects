# Plug-in Media

Every medium that plugs in by a contact edge, as one parametric **archetype**: a
shell split on its mid-plane around a board whose tongue reaches a contact window,
with label recesses, generic grip ridges, keying notches, a shutter door, a
pass-through slot, and a card mode for monolithic thin cards. It spans the
contact-edge media from a 1976 cartridge with a door to a current game card.
CadQuery (B-Rep) reference.

Named formats ride as **generated presets**. Read [`../NOTICE`](../NOTICE) first:
a preset is a fit envelope, not a likeness, and the family reproduces no trade
dress. A label is a recess, never art.

## Frame — read this first

| Model | Catalog field (`plug-in-media`, kind `media`) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `shell_width` | `dimensions_mm.states.shell.width` | X, across the label face, centred on `x = 0` |
| `shell_height` | `dimensions_mm.states.shell.height` | Z, up the label face; the shell stands on `z = 0` |
| `shell_thickness` | `dimensions_mm.states.shell.depth` | Y, through the shell; the label (front) face is −Y, the split plane `y = 0` |
| `insertion_edge` | `features.insertion_edge` | the label edge carrying the contacts: `bottom`, `top`, or `side` (+X) |
| `edge_contacts`, `edge_pitch` | `features.edge_contacts`, `features.contact_pitch_mm` | total fingers (split across both faces; a card's are on the back face) and their pitch |
| `card_mode` | `features.card` | a monolithic thin card |
| `shutter` | `features.shutter` | a door over the contact window |
| `pass_through_slot` | `features.pass_through_slot` | a slot in the far edge that takes a second medium |

The shell is built with its contact edge at `z = 0` and turned into place by
`media_place`; the turn is about Y only, so the label face stays −Y and **the
envelope's bounding box never depends on the insertion edge**: it is always
`shell_width × max(shell_thickness, grip_thickness) × shell_height` (X × Y × Z).
`insert_travel` moves the whole medium toward its contact edge (the `insert`
animation; a kit's insert pose).

## Modes and parts

| Mode | Parts | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `media` | front_shell, back_shell, pcb_envelope | the assembled medium. A preview, not a print target. |
| `envelope` | envelope | the plain outer hull, **one body**: the fit surface and the box a preset asserts |
| `front_shell` | front_shell | the label half, y < 0 |
| `back_shell` | back_shell | the other half, y > 0: a card's contact pocket and the shutter outline |
| `pcb_envelope` | pcb_envelope | the board, its tongue and its edge contacts (card mode: the contact plate). A keep-out, not a circuit. |

Every (mode, part) is one body by design. Animation: `insert` (`insert_travel`
0 → 40 mm, mode `media`, 6 frames; a pure translation, so every frame renders).

## Presets — where every number comes from

Presets are **generated** by `tools/presets/presets.py` (module
`tools/presets/plug_in_media.py`) from the keystone's catalog at `SPEC_PIN`; CI runs
`--check-all`. A sourced preset sets the shell from `states.shell` and the
features the catalog covers; every other group falls back to the archetype, and
the preset's `registry.archetype_fallbacks` names each one. An object with an
unresolved shell gets no preset and is listed in `_generated_from.awaiting_a_source`.

**At first publication the pinned catalog files no `plug-in-media` object**
(research lane RA4 is filing them). The family therefore ships five archetype
presets, `kind: archetype`, which assert nothing about any product:

| Preset | W × H × D (mm) | Contact edge | What it exercises |
|---|---|---|---|
| `archetype-top-loading-cartridge` (= the default) | 100 × 110 × 15, band 19 | bottom | hollow shell, grip band, ridges |
| `archetype-front-loading-cartridge` | 120 × 130 × 17 | top | the 180° turn, two keying notches |
| `archetype-card` | 40 × 30 × 3 | side | card mode, the −90° turn, one notch |
| `archetype-pass-through-cartridge` | 110 × 130 × 22 | bottom | the far-edge slot; the board stops below it |
| `archetype-shuttered-cartridge` | 105 × 120 × 20 | bottom | the shutter door outline |

## Archetype rules (ours, `tools/presets/plug_in_media.py`)

- Corner radius 8 % of the shorter side, 0.5–4 mm. Wall 11 % of the thickness,
  0.8–2 mm. Board 1.6 mm; a card's contact plate 25 % of its thickness, 0.3–0.8 mm.
  Clearance 0.3 mm; board edge 1 mm in from the contact edge.
- Contact window: with a sourced count and pitch, `ceil(count / 2) × pitch + 4 mm`
  (a card: `count × pitch + 4 mm`); otherwise 80 % of the contact edge, with as
  many fingers as fit at 2.5 mm (a card: 1.2 mm). Depth 9 % of the length, 4–12 mm
  (a card: 20 %, 3–8 mm). A count that cannot fit the edge is reported, never
  squeezed.
- A shell 4 mm thick or thinner is a card when the catalog does not say.
- Grip band (archetypes only) 20 % of the length; 5 ridges at 3 mm pitch, 0.6 mm
  deep (or 40 % of the wall) at the far edge; cards carry none.
- Labels: front 80 % of the width, filling the face between the window and the
  grip with 3 mm margins; back 60 % of the width, just above the window (or the
  shutter). Depth 0.6 mm (40 % of the wall), a card 0.3 mm.
- Notches 8 % of the length wide, 4 % of the width deep, 15 % of the length from
  the contact edge. Shutter door: window plus a wall each side, window depth
  + 6 mm long, 0.6 mm groove. Pass-through slot 70 % of the edge, 55 % of the
  thickness, 15 % of the length deep.
- Slider ranges are typed to cover the archetype extremes ±20 % with room for
  the span of the history; they move to generator-written reference extremes
  once the catalog files objects.
